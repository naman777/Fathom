## Overview

Founded in 1968, the Ashcombe Institute of Technology (AIT) is a prestigious institution located in Ashcombe, New Hampshire. Renowned for its rigorous academic programs and a focus on technological innovation, AIT offers a wide range of undergraduate and graduate programs across multiple disciplines. The institute's mission is to foster an environment conducive to research, collaboration, and technological advancement. 

### Key Statistics
- **Founding Year:** 1968
- **Location:** Ashcombe, New Hampshire
- **Current Enrollment:** Approximately 12,000 students
- **Campus Size:** 250 acres
- **Motto:** "Innovating for Tomorrow"
  
## Colleges and Programs

AIT is organized into five primary colleges, each specializing in distinct areas of study:

### 1. College of Engineering and Technology
- **Programs Offered:** 
  - Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering
  - Bachelor of Information Technology
  - Master's in Cybersecurity
- **Notable Research Center:** Center for Advanced Robotics and Automation (CARA)
  
### 2. College of Arts and Sciences
- **Programs Offered:**
  - Bachelor of Arts in Digital Media
  - Bachelor of Science in Environmental Science
  - Master's in Applied Psychology
- **Notable Research Center:** Institute for Social Innovation (ISI)
  
### 3. College of Business and Management
- **Programs Offered:**
  - Bachelor of Business Administration
  - Master of Business Administration (MBA)
- **Notable Research Center:** Ashcombe Center for Entrepreneurship (ACE)
  
### 4. College of Health Sciences
- **Programs Offered:**
  - Bachelor of Science in Nursing
  - Master of Public Health
- **Notable Research Center:** Center for Health Policy Research (CHPR)
  
### 5. College of Education
- **Programs Offered:**
  - Bachelor of Arts in Educational Studies
  - Master of Arts in Curriculum and Instruction
- **Notable Research Center:** Educational Innovation Research Center (EIRC)

## Tuition and Financial Aid

For the academic year 2023-2024, the tuition fees at AIT are set as follows:

- **Undergraduate Tuition:** $54,000 per year
- **Graduate Tuition:** $45,000 per year
- **Out-of-State Fees:** Additional $5,000 for non-New Hampshire residents

### Financial Aid
AIT offers a comprehensive financial aid program, including scholarships, grants, and work-study opportunities. Approximately 65% of the student body receives some form of financial aid.

- **Endowed Scholarships:** AIT has over 150 endowed scholarships funded by alumni and benefactors. Notable among them is the **James and Linda Wilcox Scholarship**, which awards up to $10,000 annually to deserving students pursuing engineering degrees.
  
- **Federal Aid:** Students may also qualify for federal financial aid programs, including Pell Grants and Federal Direct Loans, subject to prior eligibility assessments.

## Research Centers

AIT is home to a number of renowned research centers that contribute to its reputation as a leader in technological innovation and development. These centers collaborate with industry partners and government entities to push the boundaries of knowledge.

### Center for Advanced Robotics and Automation (CARA)
Founded in 2012, CARA focuses on autonomous systems and robotics. The center received a $5 million grant from the **U.S. Department of Defense** in 2023 to advance research in military robotics applications.

### Institute for Social Innovation (ISI)
Established in 2015, ISI conducts research on social entrepreneurship and community development. In 2021, the institute was awarded a $3 million grant from the **National Science Foundation** to study the impact of technology in underserved communities.

### Ashcombe Center for Entrepreneurship (ACE)
Launched in 2000, ACE supports startup ventures and entrepreneurial initiatives. Notably, the center runs the **Ashcombe Business Incubator**, which has successfully launched over 250 startups since its inception.

## Endowment

As of the latest fiscal year ended June 2023, the AIT endowment stands at approximately **$800 million**, making it one of the wealthiest institutions in the Northeast. The substantial endowment allows AIT to invest in both academic programming and infrastructure improvements.

### Annual Growth
The endowment has seen an average annual return of **8.5%** over the past decade, facilitated by a diversified investment strategy that includes equities, fixed income, and alternative assets.

### Notable Donors
Prominent contributions have come from notable alumni such as:
- **Dr. Richard P. Hargrove**, Class of 1982, who donated $50 million for the establishment of the Hargrove Center for Innovation.
- **Ms. Eliza Montgomery**, Class of 1990, who established a $10 million scholarship fund for female undergraduates in STEM fields.

## Campus Policies

AIT is committed to providing a safe and inclusive campus environment. Key policies include:

### 1. Title IX Compliance
AIT is vigilant in its commitment to preventing and addressing sexual harassment and discrimination. The Title IX Office offers training programs and resources for students and staff.

### 2. Academic Integrity Policy
AIT enforces a strict academic integrity policy, with zero tolerance for cheating or plagiarism. Violations can result in disciplinary action, including expulsion.

### 3. Sustainability Initiatives
In 2020, AIT implemented a comprehensive sustainability policy aimed at reducing the campus carbon footprint by 50% by 2030. This includes initiatives such as zero-waste dining and energy-efficient building upgrades.

### 4. Mental Health Services
The Institute provides extensive mental health resources, including the **Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS)**, which offers workshops, counseling, and crisis intervention.

The Ashcombe Institute of Technology stands as a beacon of academic excellence, innovation, and community engagement. With a commitment to research, student success, and societal impact, AIT continues to prepare future leaders and thinkers for the challenges of the modern world.
