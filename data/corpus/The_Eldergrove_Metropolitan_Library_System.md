# The Eldergrove Metropolitan Library System: A Comprehensive Overview

## Introduction

Established in 1975, the Eldergrove Metropolitan Library System (EMLS) serves the expansive Eldergrove region, encompassing the cities of Eldergrove, Maplewood, and Oakridge. With a mission to enhance community engagement and lifelong learning, EMLS operates 12 branches and hosts an array of diverse programs, collections, and services. As of 2023, the library system serves over 300,000 residents and boasts a collection exceeding 1.5 million items.

## Branches

### 1. Main Branch - Eldergrove Central Library

**Location:** 123 Library Lane, Eldergrove, CA  
**Square Footage:** 75,000 sq. ft.  
**Established:** 1975  
**Annual Visitors:** 600,000

The Eldergrove Central Library serves as the flagship location of EMLS, featuring a state-of-the-art auditorium, extensive meeting rooms, and a dedicated children's area known as "The Imagination Station." The branch houses over 500,000 books, periodicals, and digital resources.

### 2. Maplewood Community Library

**Location:** 456 Maple St., Maplewood, CA  
**Square Footage:** 35,000 sq. ft.  
**Established:** 1992  
**Annual Visitors:** 200,000

Maplewood Community Library focuses on community-driven programs, offering workshops in arts and crafts, computer literacy, and English as a second language (ESL). The collection includes 150,000 items, with a special emphasis on local history.

### 3. Oakridge Branch Library

**Location:** 789 Oakridge Blvd., Oakridge, CA  
**Square Footage:** 25,000 sq. ft.  
**Established:** 1985  
**Annual Visitors:** 150,000

The Oakridge Branch is known for its tranquil reading garden and children's storytelling theater. The branch features 75,000 items and regularly hosts family movie nights.

### Additional Branches

In addition to the main library and the two community branches, EMLS comprises the following libraries:

- Silver Birch Library (Established 1998)
- Redwood Heights Library (Established 2001)
- Crystal Lake Library (Established 2010)
- Willow Creek Library (Established 2015)
- Heritage Park Library (Established 2020)
- Pine Valley Library (Established 2022)
- North End Library (Established 2023)
- Future Branch: West Eldergrove Library (Projected opening 2025)

## Collections

### Overview of Collections

As of 2023, EMLS supports a diverse range of collections, categorized into several key areas:

- **Books:** 1,200,000+ items
- **Digital Media:** 250,000 eBooks, audiobooks, and databases
- **Periodicals:** 55,000 print and digital subscriptions
- **Local Archives:** 25,000 historical documents, photographs, and artifacts

### Special Collections

#### Local History Collection

Located primarily at the Eldergrove Central Library, this collection focuses on the rich history of Eldergrove and its surrounding areas, preserving artifacts dating back to the city’s founding in 1845. 

#### Children's Collection

The children's collection encompasses educational resources, fiction, and multimedia, totaling over 300,000 items. "The Imagination Station" includes interactive learning installations to enrich the literacy experience for young readers.

#### Technology Collection

EMLS maintains a robust technology collection, with over 2,000 devices available for checkout, including tablets, laptops, and STEM kits aimed at promoting digital literacy.

## Budgets

### Annual Budget Overview for 2023

The total operating budget for EMLS in 2023 stands at approximately $15 million, with funding allocated across various sectors:

- **Personnel:** $8 million (53% of the budget)
- **Collections:** $3 million (20% of the budget)
- **Facilities Maintenance:** $2 million (13% of the budget)
- **Programs and Services:** $1 million (7% of the budget)
- **Technology Initiatives:** $1 million (7% of the budget)

### Funding Sources

The library system's funding is provided by multiple sources:

- **Local Government Grants:** 65%
- **State Funding:** 20%
- **Donations and Grants:** 10%
- **Fines and Fees:** 5%

## Programs

### Educational Programs

#### Lifelong Learning Initiative

Launched in 2021, the Lifelong Learning Initiative offers free workshops and classes on various subjects, including technology, literature, and health. In 2023, over 5,000 participants enrolled in more than 150 different sessions.

#### Summer Reading Program

The EMLS Summer Reading Program encourages reading among children during the summer months, resulting in over 15,000 registrations in 2023 alone. Participants can earn prizes based on their reading goals.

### Community Outreach Programs

#### Mobile Library Services

In 2022, EMLS initiated a Mobile Library Service, utilizing a bookmobile that visits underserved neighborhoods bi-weekly, extending library access to over 5,000 residents who lack nearby branch access.

#### Cultural Events

EMLS hosts numerous cultural events annually, including "Eldergrove Cultural Festival," which celebrates local diversity, attracting over 10,000 attendees each year.

## Policies

### Collection Development Policy

The EMLS collection development policy emphasizes diversity and accessibility, ensuring materials reflect the community's demographics and interests. The library regularly reviews and updates collections based on patron feedback and usage statistics.

### Borrowing Policy

Patrons are permitted to borrow up to 50 items simultaneously, with a lending period of three weeks for most materials. Digital materials may have varied borrowing periods based on licensing agreements.

### Public Access Policy

All EMLS branches maintain an open-access policy, allowing free entry to the public. Facilities are equipped with ADA-compliant features to ensure accessibility for individuals with disabilities.

## Conclusion

The Eldergrove Metropolitan Library System remains dedicated to serving its community through extensive resources, innovative programs, and a commitment to lifelong learning. By continually adapting to the needs of its patrons, EMLS is poised to foster a love of reading and education in Eldergrove for years to come.
